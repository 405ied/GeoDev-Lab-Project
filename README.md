# GIS-Based Multi-Criteria Assessment of Border Outpost Suitability and Terrain Accessibility Along the Nigeria–Benin Border in Ogun State, Nigeria

By integrating spatial planning principles from Urban and Regional Planning with advanced Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and Remote Sensing techniques, this research establishes an objective, evidence-based spatial decision-support framework tailored specifically for border management agencies such as the Nigeria Immigration Service.

**GeoDev Lab Africa, Cohort One.** Akeem Lasisi

---

## The question

> The geographic focus of this study is the western international boundary corridor shared between Nigeria and the Republic of Benin, specifically within Ogun State. The study area encompasses critical border local government areas including Ipokia, Imeko Afon, Yewa South, and Yewa North.
This corridor features a dynamic geographical and infrastructural mix, ranging from formal transit hubs like Idiroko to dense forest covers, rural agricultural settlements, and porous unofficial tracks, providing an ideal landscape for spatial vulnerability and suitability analysis.

## How to run it

```bash

cd GIS-Based Multi-Criteria Assessment of Border Outpost Suitability and Terrain Accessibility Along the Nigeria–Benin Border in Ogun State, Nigeria
```
